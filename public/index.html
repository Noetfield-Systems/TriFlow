BUILT AROUND YOUR VOICE

Three languages. One flow.

TriFlow is a standalone voice studio: three languages in one microphone connection, live transcriptions, natural translations and expressive AI replies.

Private by defaultTranscript history stays in your browser unless you export it. Audio is processed by the configured speech provider during live sessions.
Device voice, no extra voice billSpoken replies use the free voices already available on your device. Quality and language coverage vary. SourceB’s chat technology uses Cloudflare Workers AI with a limited free allocation. Its Retell receptionist is metered and does not provide automatic three-language switching out of the box.
No recording archiveTriFlow does not record or store raw audio on its server.
